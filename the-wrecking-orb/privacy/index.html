Privacy Policy

The Wrecking Orb · Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy explains how Tony Sanginez (Jogabit) ("we", "us") handles information in the game The Wrecking Orb for Android ("the game").

1. Information stored by the game

Your progress (scores, essence, upgrades, skins, achievements and settings) is saved only on your device. We do not run servers and we do not collect your game progress.

2. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device information and ad interaction data in order to serve ads, measure their performance and prevent fraud.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game asks for your consent before showing personalized ads, using Google's consent platform. You can change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy.

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

3. In-app purchases (Samsung)

The optional "Remove ads" purchase is processed by Samsung In-App Purchase through Galaxy Store. Samsung handles the payment. We never receive your card details or account credentials. Samsung's own privacy policy applies to that processing.

4. Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

5. Your choices

Because we do not store personal data on our systems, you can delete all game information by uninstalling the game or by using Settings → Reset progress. For data processed by Google, use Google's privacy tools.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The current version will always be published on this page with its date.
